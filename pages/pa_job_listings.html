About This Job Board

I plan on updating this job board on a weekly basis. Over time, I also plan to build visualizations and run analyses to better understand hiring trends in people analytics. This is not a complete list of every people analytics job in the world. I primarily use job titles to decide what to include, which means that some real people analytics roles may be missing and some roles listed here might not truly be people analytics roles.

I try my best to classify each role by Work mode (on-site, hybrid, remote), Location (country/state), and Job level (intern, individual contributor, manager, director+). I also flag new listings. These fields may not always be 100% accurate. I also record the date I pulled the job, which is not always the same as the date the job was originally posted.

Filters and Sorting

You can filter by work mode, job level, region, and newly added roles. You can also sort the list by each column.
