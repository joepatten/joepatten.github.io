About These Trends

These charts visualize hiring trends in the people analytics space, drawn from the same data that powers the PA Job Listings. Use the filters to explore active job counts over time by country, work mode, and job level.

The number of active listings shown here will not always match the total number of rows in the job table, and it is often lower. For trend aggregation, I combine postings that appear to represent the same role across multiple work arrangements. For example, if the same job is posted separately as remote and hybrid or on-site, I keep those as separate rows in the table but count them as one role in this chart.
