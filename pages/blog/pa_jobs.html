About This Job Board

This job board contains people analytics job listings for the US, the UK, Australia, and Canada (if you want me to add more countries to the board, please let me know). I plan on updating this job board on a weekly basis. Over time, I also plan to build visualizations and run analyses to better understand hiring trends in people analytics. This is not a complete list of every people analytics job in the world. I primarily use job titles to decide what to include, which means that some real people analytics roles may be missing and some roles listed here might not truly be people analytics roles.

I try my best to classify each role by Work mode (on-site, hybrid, remote), Location (country/state), and Job level (intern, individual contributor, manager, director+). I also flag new listings. These fields may not always be 100% accurate. I also record the date I pulled the job, which is not always the same as the date the job was originally posted.

Filters and Sorting

You can filter jobs by work mode, job level, region, and newly added roles. Use the search bar to filter across all fields with keywords like “California” (to find jobs in California) or “Amazon” (to find jobs at Amazon). You can also sort by any column - hold Shift to sort by multiple columns.

Want to Add a Role?
