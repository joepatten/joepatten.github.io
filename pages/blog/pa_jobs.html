The People's People Analytics Job Listings

About These Job Listings

This semi-curated list includes job listings for people analytics roles in the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia (if you’d like me to add other countries, feel free to reach out). I plan on updating this on a weekly basis. Over time, I also plan to build visualizations and run analyses to better understand hiring trends in the people analytics space. This is not an exhaustive list of every people analytics job available. I mainly use job titles to determine inclusion, so some relevant roles may be missing and a few listed roles listed in this job board may not be strictly people analytics.

I classify each role by work mode (on-site, hybrid, remote), location (country/state), and job level (intern, individual contributor, manager, director+), and I flag newly added listings. These fields may not always be perfectly accurate. I also record the date the job was pulled, which may differ from the original posting date.

Filters and Sorting

You can filter jobs by work mode, job level, region, and newly added roles. Additionally, you can use the search bar to filter across all fields with keywords like California (to find jobs in California), Amazon (to find jobs at Amazon), or analyst (to find all jobs with analyst in the job title). You can also sort by any column - hold Shift to sort by multiple columns.

Want to Add a Role?

If you’d like me to add a role, just email me at: joempatten@gmail.com with the job posting url (preferably from the company's hire portal). This is very much a work in progress, so if you find any bugs or have ideas for modifications, please feel free to reach out. Happy hunting, and let me know how I can help!
